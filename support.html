常見問題

按播放沒有聲音?

先點一下畫面(iPhone 要在點擊之後才會開始出聲),再確認音量。網頁版第一次播放要先從網路下載鋼琴音色,下載完才會有聲音;App 版的鋼琴音色內建在 App 裡。iPhone 側邊的靜音開關打開時 App 照樣有聲音;如果還是沒聲音,請回報並附上裝置型號。

不連網可以用嗎?

App 版:可以,第一次打開就不需要網路。網頁版:連網打開過一次、鋼琴音色下載過之後,就能離線使用。

檢定的要求跟官方一樣嗎?

音階琶音、聽力照 ABRSM《Piano Practical Grades 2025 & 2026》與 Trinity《Piano Syllabus from 2023》整理;視奏題目是 App 依 ABRSM 官方視奏範例整理的規則產生的練習題,不是官方題目(Trinity 與 ABRSM 初級的視奏還沒有規則,所以沒有出題)。僅供練習,實際要求以官方大綱為準。本 App 與 ABRSM、Trinity 沒有任何關係。

麥克風會錄音嗎?

視奏的「跟著彈」可以選用麥克風評節奏:只有你勾選並允許之後才會用,聲音只在這台裝置上分析起音時間,不錄音、不上傳。不勾選就完全不會用到麥克風。

練習紀錄存在哪裡?會上傳嗎?

只存在這台裝置上,不會上傳;刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。詳見隱私權政策。

怎麼換考試、級數,或不考了?

打開「檢定」分頁,在上面的考試設定選考試系統與級數,或選「不考試」。

FAQ

No sound when I press play?

Tap the screen once (iPhone only starts audio after a tap) and check the volume. The web version downloads the piano sound the first time you play; the app has it built in. The app still plays with the iPhone's silent switch on; if you still hear nothing, please report it with your device model.

Does it work offline?

App: yes, from the very first launch. Web: after you have opened it online once and the piano sound has downloaded.

Are the exam requirements official?

Scales, arpeggios and aural follow ABRSM Piano Practical Grades 2025 & 2026 and the Trinity Piano Syllabus from 2023. Sight-reading tests are generated by the app from rules modelled on ABRSM specimen tests — they are not official tests (there are no rules yet for Trinity or ABRSM Initial sight-reading, so none are generated). Practice only — the official syllabus is final. This app is not affiliated with ABRSM or Trinity.

Does the microphone record me?

Sight-reading's play-along can optionally score your rhythm with the microphone. It is used only after you tick it and allow access; the sound is analysed on this device for note start times only, never recorded or uploaded. If you don't tick it, the microphone is never used.

Where is my practice history kept? Is it uploaded?

Only on this device, never uploaded; it is removed when you delete the app or clear site data. See the privacy policy.

How do I change exam or grade, or stop exam prep?

Open the Exams tab and pick a board and grade at the top, or choose "No exam".

How do I switch language or light/dark theme?

The two buttons at the right of the top bar.
