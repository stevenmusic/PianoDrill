隱私權政策

PianoDrill 鋼琴檢定特訓 · 最後更新 2026-11-06

我們不收集任何個人資料

PianoDrill 不需要註冊、不需要登入,沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤,也不會把任何資料傳到我們的伺服器(我們沒有伺服器)。

存在你裝置上的資料

  • 練習設定、考試級數、通過標記、答題正確率、出過的視奏題目與練習紀錄:只存在這台裝置的本機儲存空間,刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。
  • 頁尾的「備份紀錄」存下來的檔案、「分享本週摘要」的文字:只在你的裝置上產生,由你自己決定存在哪裡、傳給誰;App 不會上傳。
  • 鋼琴取樣音檔:App 版已內建在 App 裡,不需要連網。網頁版第一次播放時從 GitHub(raw.githubusercontent.com)下載,之後存在裝置上供離線使用;下載時 GitHub 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址等),適用 GitHub 的隱私權政策。

網頁版的字型

網頁版從 Google Fonts(fonts.googleapis.com、fonts.gstatic.com)載入字型(Noto Sans TC、Noto Serif TC),載入時 Google 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址、瀏覽器等),適用 Google 的隱私權政策。App 版不載入 Google Fonts,改用裝置內建的字型,不會連到 Google。

麥克風(視奏與今日練習)

視奏按「彈一遍」時,App 用麥克風聽你彈,彈完自動打分(節奏、穩定、連貫;初級到 2 級另外看該彈的音有沒有出現)。今日練習的部分關卡按 ▶「你彈一輪」時,App 也用麥克風聽你有沒有彈對,彈對就自動算「做到了」。第一次真的要聽時系統才會問你要不要允許;不允許也可以照常練習,只是不打分、改成自己按「做到了」。

聲音只在這台裝置上分析,不錄音存檔、不上傳,打完分就丟掉。不想用可以關掉視奏的「用麥克風自動打分」與今日練習的「麥克風聽你彈」,兩個都關掉以後完全不會用到麥克風。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡,不收集兒童的任何資料。

回報問題

App 不會自動傳送任何資料(沒有當機回報、沒有分析)。在支援頁按「回報」時,才會打開預先填好 App 版本、裝置、語言的 GitHub 表單或郵件,你看過、自己按送出才會送出;不包含練習紀錄。GitHub 上的 issue 是公開的。

聯絡

問題或建議請看支援頁,或到 GitHub:github.com/stevenmusic/PianoDrill

Privacy Policy

PianoDrill · Last updated 2026-11-06

We collect no personal data

PianoDrill needs no account or sign-in, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and sends nothing to any server of ours (there is none).

Data stored on your device

  • Settings, your exam and grade, pass marks, accuracy stats, sight-reading tests already seen and the practice log are kept only in this device's local storage and are removed when you delete the app or clear site data.
  • The backup file from “Backup” and the text from “Share this week” (at the bottom of the page) are made on your device; you choose where to save them and whom to send them to. The app uploads nothing.
  • Piano samples are built into the app and need no connection. The web version downloads them from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) the first time they are played and caches them on the device for offline use; GitHub sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Fonts on the web version

The web version loads its fonts (Noto Sans TC, Noto Serif TC) from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), so Google sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address and browser) under its own privacy policy. The app does not load Google Fonts; it uses the fonts built into your device and never connects to Google.

Microphone (sight-reading and Today)

When you tap “Play it” in sight-reading, the app listens through the microphone and scores you when you finish (rhythm, steadiness, flow; for Initial to Grade 2 also whether the written notes were played). In Today, some levels also listen when you tap ▶ “Your turn” to check whether you played the notes; if you did, it counts as “Did it” automatically. Your device asks for permission the first time the app really needs to listen; if you decline, you can still practise, just without a score, tapping “Did it” yourself.

The sound is analysed on this device only, never recorded to a file or uploaded, and discarded once scored. Switch off “Score me with the microphone” in sight-reading and “Mic listens” in Today and the microphone is never used.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.

Reporting problems

The app never sends anything by itself (no crash reporting, no analytics). Only when you press Report on the support page does it open a GitHub form or email draft pre-filled with the app version, device and language; nothing is sent until you review it and send it yourself, and no practice history is included. GitHub issues are public.

Contact

Questions or suggestions: see the support page or github.com/stevenmusic/PianoDrill
