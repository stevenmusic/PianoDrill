隱私權政策

PianoDrill 鋼琴檢定特訓 · 最後更新 2026-10-16

我們不收集任何個人資料

PianoDrill 不需要註冊、不需要登入,沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤,也不會把任何資料傳到我們的伺服器(我們沒有伺服器)。

存在你裝置上的資料

  • 練習設定、考試級數、通過標記、答題正確率、出過的視奏題目與練習紀錄:只存在這台裝置的本機儲存空間,刪除 App 或清除網站資料就會一起刪除。
  • 鋼琴取樣音檔:第一次播放時從 GitHub(raw.githubusercontent.com)下載,之後存在裝置上供離線使用。下載時 GitHub 會看到一般的網路連線資訊(IP 位址等),適用 GitHub 的隱私權政策。

麥克風(選用)

視奏的「跟著彈」可以選擇用麥克風評分節奏。只有你勾選並允許之後才會使用;聲音只在這台裝置上分析起音的時間,不錄音存檔、不上傳,彈完就丟掉。不勾選就完全不會用到麥克風。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡,不收集兒童的任何資料。

Privacy Policy

PianoDrill · Last updated 2026-10-16

We collect no personal data

PianoDrill needs no account or sign-in, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and sends nothing to any server of ours (there is none).

Data stored on your device

  • Settings, your exam and grade, pass marks, accuracy stats, sight-reading tests already seen and the practice log are kept only in this device's local storage and are removed when you delete the app or clear site data.
  • Piano samples are downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) the first time they are played and cached on the device for offline use. GitHub sees ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Microphone (optional)

Sight-reading's play-along can optionally score your rhythm with the microphone. It is used only after you tick that option and allow access; the sound is analysed on this device for note start times only, never recorded to a file or uploaded, and discarded when you finish. If you don't tick it, the microphone is never used.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.
